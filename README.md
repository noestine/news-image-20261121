  jet-powered drone crash in south-eastern Poland during a huge overnight

- **Denmark foils plot** — Danish police reveal they foiled a Russian

  attache.
- **Riyadh intercepts 3 missiles** — the Saudi-led coalition shoots down
  three Houthi missiles over Riyadh and strikes Hodeidah, a day after the
  PIF Tower was hit.
- **YouTube nears $3bn deal** — an in-principle agreement with thousands
